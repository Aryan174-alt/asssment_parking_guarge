Everything You Need

Purpose-built tools for busy parking attendants and facility managers.

⚡

Instant Check-In / Check-Out

Enter a license plate and vehicle type — the system auto-assigns the best available spot. Check-out calculates the fee instantly based on tiered pricing rules.

🔒

Concurrency-Safe Booking

Database-level partial unique indexes guarantee that no spot is ever double-booked, even when two attendants check in simultaneously.

🔋

EV Spot Enforcement

EV vehicles are routed exclusively to charger-equipped spots. Regular vehicles are never placed in EV spots, keeping chargers available.

💰

Smart Tiered Pricing

First hour at a premium rate, subsequent hours at a discount, with a daily cap. Partial hours always round up — no revenue lost.

🔍

Vehicle Lookup

Search any plate number to instantly see if the vehicle is currently parked, which spot it's in, and its complete visit history.

📊

Paginated History

Browse through thousands of past tickets with full pagination and sortable columns for check-in time and fee amount.
